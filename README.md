Bubble Chart Analysis

The Bubble Chart compares the sales performance of different product sub-categories. Each bubble represents a sub-category, while the size of the bubble represents total Sales.
Larger bubbles indicate sub-categories with higher sales, whereas smaller bubbles indicate comparatively lower sales.
The color represents the main product category: Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
This visualization makes it easy to compare the relative contribution of different sub-categories to overall business sales.

Five Business Insights

1.Technology has the highest category sales, with approximately 836,154, compared with Furniture at 742,000 and Office Supplies at 719,047.
2.West is the highest-performing region, generating approximately 725,458 in sales.
3.South has the lowest regional sales, at approximately 391,722, indicating a noticeable difference in sales across regions.
4.The Bubble Chart shows significant variation between sub-categories. Larger bubbles represent sub-categories contributing more sales, while smaller bubbles represent lower sales contributors.
5.The Sales Trend visualization shows that sales performance changes over time, and the three product categories show different patterns across the period.


Two Business Recommendations:

Recommendation 1

The business should maintain sufficient inventory and marketing support for high-performing Technology products and other high-sales sub-categories to sustain their contribution to overall sales.

Recommendation 2

The business should investigate the lower sales performance of the South region and lower-performing sub-categories to identify opportunities involving product availability, pricing, promotion, or customer demand.
